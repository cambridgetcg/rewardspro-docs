# Marketing Campaigns

Leverage your loyalty program data to create targeted marketing campaigns that drive engagement and repeat purchases.

## Campaign Types

### Tier-Based Campaigns

Target customers based on their loyalty tier:

| Campaign | Target | Goal |
|----------|--------|------|
| VIP Exclusive | Platinum only | Reward top customers |
| Upgrade Push | Silver members | Encourage Gold qualification |
| Win-Back | Bronze (inactive) | Re-engage lapsed customers |

### Spending-Based Campaigns

Target customers based on spending patterns:

- **High Spenders** - Customers with $1,000+ lifetime value
- **Approaching Threshold** - Within $100 of next tier
- **Recent Big Purchase** - Placed large order in past 30 days

### Balance-Based Campaigns

Target customers based on store credit:

- **Has Balance** - Remind to use available credit
- **Low Balance** - Encourage purchases to earn more
- **Expiring Soon** - (If credit expiration is enabled)

## Segmentation

### Export Customer Segments

Export customer lists for use in email marketing platforms:

1. Go to **Customers**
2. Apply filters (tier, balance, spend)
3. Click **Export**
4. Import to Klaviyo, Mailchimp, etc.

### Available Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Email | Customer email address |
| Name | Full name |
| Tier | Current loyalty tier |
| Balance | Store credit balance |
| Lifetime Spend | Total amount spent |
| Join Date | When they joined the program |

## Shopify Flow Integration (Coming Soon)

Automate campaigns using Shopify Flow triggers:

### Available Triggers

- `tier.upgraded` - Customer moved to higher tier
- `tier.downgraded` - Customer moved to lower tier
- `credit.earned` - Cashback was awarded
- `credit.redeemed` - Store credit was used
- `milestone.reached` - Spending milestone hit

### Example Flows

**Tier Upgrade Celebration**
```
Trigger: Customer upgraded to Gold
Wait: 1 hour
Action: Send "Congratulations on Gold!" email
Action: Add tag "gold-member"
```

**Milestone Bonus**
```
Trigger: Customer reaches $1,000 lifetime spend
Action: Add $25 bonus store credit
Action: Send "Milestone Reward" email
```

**Re-engagement Campaign**
```
Trigger: 60 days since last purchase
Condition: Customer has store credit balance
Action: Send "Your credit is waiting" email
```

## Campaign Ideas

### New Member Welcome Series

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0 | Welcome email explaining program |
| 3 | Tips to earn faster |
| 7 | First purchase reminder |
| 14 | Highlight tier benefits |

### Tier Upgrade Push

Target customers close to next tier:

1. Identify customers within 20% of threshold
2. Send personalized "You're almost there!" message
3. Show exactly how much more they need to spend
4. Offer bonus points for purchases this week

### VIP Appreciation

Keep your best customers engaged:

- Early access to sales
- Exclusive product previews
- Birthday/anniversary bonuses
- Surprise credit bonuses

### Win-Back Campaign

Re-engage inactive members:

1. Identify customers inactive 60+ days
2. Segment by tier (different messaging)
3. Offer bonus credit for return purchase
4. Remind them of their current balance

## Measuring Success

Track campaign effectiveness:

| Metric | How to Measure |
|--------|----------------|
| Open Rate | Email platform analytics |
| Conversion | Orders from campaign recipients |
| Revenue | Total sales attributed to campaign |
| Tier Upgrades | Customers who upgraded after campaign |
| Credit Usage | Redemption rate post-campaign |

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- Personalize with tier and name
- Include store credit balance in emails
- Show progress to next tier
- Make CTAs clear and urgent
- Test different send times

### Don'ts ❌
- Over-communicate (max 2-3 emails/week)
- Send generic messages
- Ignore segmentation opportunities
- Forget mobile optimization
- Neglect to track results
