## Free Plan

Perfect for new or small stores testing loyalty programs.

**Includes:**
- Up to 100 orders/month
- Unlimited tiers
- Basic analytics
- Customer widget
- Email support

**Limitations:**
- No tier products
- Basic analytics only
- Standard support

## Starter Plan - $19/month

Great for growing stores with moderate volume.

**Everything in Free, plus:**
- Up to 500 orders/month
- Tier products
- Full analytics
- Priority email support

## Growth Plan - $49/month

For established stores with significant order volume.

**Everything in Starter, plus:**
- Up to 2,000 orders/month
- Subscription billing
- API access
- Advanced analytics
- Chat support

## Enterprise Plan - $99/month

For high-volume stores needing premium features.

**Everything in Growth, plus:**
- Unlimited orders
- Priority support
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated success manager
- SLA guarantee

## Billing

### Payment Methods

- Credit/debit card (via Shopify)
- Shopify billing integration

### Billing Cycle

- Charged monthly through Shopify
- Pro-rated for plan changes
- Usage tracked in real-time

### Order Counting

Orders are counted when:
- Payment is confirmed
- Processed for cashback

Not counted:
- Cancelled orders
- Test orders
- Draft orders

## Upgrading

### When to Upgrade

Consider upgrading if:
- Approaching order limit
- Need advanced features
- Want priority support

### How to Upgrade

1. Go to **Billing** in RewardsPro
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**
3. Select new plan
4. Confirm through Shopify

### Pro-Rating

- Upgrade: Pay difference immediately
- Downgrade: Credit applied to next bill

## FAQ

### What happens if I exceed my order limit?

You'll be notified and prompted to upgrade. Processing continues but you should upgrade promptly.

### Can I downgrade my plan?

Yes, downgrades take effect at next billing cycle.

### Is there a free trial?

The Free plan serves as your trial. Upgrade when ready.

### Do you offer annual billing?

Contact us for annual billing discounts.
