## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: How long does it take for cashback to appear?**
A: In automatic mode, cashback is credited within seconds of order payment. In manual mode, it depends on your review process.

**Q: Can customers see their store credit balance?**
A: Yes, through the customer account widget on your storefront.

**Q: What happens if a customer returns an order?**
A: You can reverse the cashback or convert the refund to store credit.

### Tiers

**Q: How are customers assigned to tiers?**
A: Based on their total spending within the evaluation period (annual or lifetime).

**Q: Can I manually assign a customer to a tier?**
A: Yes, from the Customer Details page.

### Store Credit

**Q: Does store credit expire?**
A: This depends on your configuration. You can set expiration policies in Settings.

**Q: Can store credit be used with discount codes?**
A: Yes, store credit can be combined with other discounts.
