## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: How much does RewardsPro cost?**
A: Nothing. RewardsPro is free. Its only plan is called Free Forever. No paid plan is offered, and nothing is ever charged without your approval in Shopify. If your store was created on or after 12 May 2025, Shopify charges its own third-party transaction fee on the part of an order paid with store credit. See [Installation](../getting-started/installation.md).

**Q: I paid for an earlier RewardsPro plan. Where are my records?**
A: If you were charged for an earlier paid plan, the charge is in your Shopify bills. Email contact@rewardspro.io with any invoice, cancellation or refund question. Nothing new is charged without your approval in Shopify.

**Q: How long does it take for cashback to appear?**
A: In automatic mode, cashback is credited within seconds of order payment. In manual mode, it depends on your review process.

**Q: Can customers see their store credit balance?**
A: Yes. Signed-in customers see it in the RewardsPro widget on your storefront and in their Shopify customer account.

**Q: What happens if a customer returns an order?**
A: RewardsPro automatically takes back the refunded share of the cashback, up to the store credit the customer still holds. Credit already spent isn't taken back.

### Tiers

**Q: How are customers assigned to tiers?**
A: Based on their total spending within the evaluation period (annual or lifetime).

**Q: Can I manually assign a customer to a tier?**
A: Yes, from the Customer Details page.

### Store Credit

**Q: Does store credit expire?**
A: RewardsPro doesn't set an expiry date on the store credit it issues.

**Q: How do customers spend store credit?**
A: At checkout, signed in through Shopify customer accounts or paying with Shop Pay. Store credit isn't available with legacy customer accounts.

**Q: Can customers spend part of their store credit?**
A: No. Shopify applies the full balance as payment, and customers can't choose a partial amount.

**Q: Are points, missions, raffles or mystery boxes available?**
A: Not yet. Customers can't spend points, missions aren't live in your storefront, and raffles and mystery boxes are paused. Cashback and tiers work today.
