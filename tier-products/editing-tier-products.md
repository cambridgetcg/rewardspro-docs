# Editing Tier Products

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/TP Products page.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

The Tier Products would be automatically created as a shopify products and you can access it in the shopify's admin products page.

All fields of the Tier Products including product name, image and sales channel can be edited except SKU, which is used by Rewards Pro to identify and assign the Membership to customers accurately.
