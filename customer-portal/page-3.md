# Customer Portal Overview

The Customer Portal is how your customers interact with your loyalty program. It displays their tier status, store credit balance, and progress toward rewards.

## Portal Components

RewardsPro provides several ways for customers to view their loyalty status:

| Component | Location | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Loyalty Widget** | Storefront (floating) | Quick status view |
| **Account Page Block** | Customer account | Detailed information |
| **Order Confirmation** | Post-purchase | Cashback earned |

## What Customers See

### Tier Status

Customers can view:
- Current tier name and badge
- Tier benefits (cashback rate)
- Progress to next tier
- Spending needed to upgrade

### Store Credit Balance

- Current available balance
- Recent transactions
- How to use credit at checkout

### Rewards History

- Cashback earned per order
- Manual credits received
- Credit redemptions

## Accessing the Portal

### For Logged-In Customers

Customers who are logged into their account see:
- Full loyalty dashboard
- Complete transaction history
- Personalized tier progress

### For Guest Visitors

Guests see:
- Program overview
- Tier benefits explanation
- Prompt to create account

## Mobile Experience

The Customer Portal is fully responsive:

- ✅ Optimized for mobile screens
- ✅ Touch-friendly interactions
- ✅ Fast loading on mobile networks
- ✅ Native-feeling animations

Merchants can customize:
