# Existing tier products

A tier product connects a Shopify product or variant to a membership tier.
For an existing purchase, inspect the order, payment status, product mapping,
assigned tier and membership dates together. A product listing alone does not
prove payment or entitlement.

## Support an existing membership

- Confirm the paid order and matching product or variant before changing access.
- Check the recorded tier, start and expiry dates, and the applicable agreement.
- If access is missing or incorrect, inspect webhook processing and order history
  before making an authorized correction.
- Handle cancellation, refunds and remaining access under the recorded terms;
  retain the original transaction and adjustment history.

For recurring agreements, see [tier subscriptions](tier-subscriptions.md).
For app billing, see [existing plans](../billing/plans.md).

The former product-creation and sales playbook is [retired history](../docs/history/retired-commercial-direction-2026-09-24/advanced-features__tier-products.md.txt), not a current offer or instruction to launch memberships.
