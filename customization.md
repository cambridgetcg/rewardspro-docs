# Customization

Personalize RewardsPro to match your brand and customer experience.

## Customer Widget

### Appearance

The customer widget automatically adapts to your theme, but you can customize:

- **Colors**: Match your brand palette
- **Position**: Choose widget placement
- **Content**: Select which information to display

### Widget Sections

Toggle visibility of widget sections:

- ✅ Tier status and badge
- ✅ Store credit balance
- ✅ Progress to next tier
- ✅ Recent transactions
- ✅ Tier benefits list

### Embedding Options

#### Theme App Block
Add the widget as a block in your theme editor:
1. Go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**
2. Add the **RewardsPro Widget** block
3. Position where desired

#### App Embed
Enable the floating widget:
1. Go to **Online Store > Themes > Customize**
2. Click **App embeds**
3. Toggle **RewardsPro Widget** on

## Tier Badges

### Custom Colors

Each tier can have a unique color:
- Use hex codes (#FFD700 for gold)
- Choose colors that convey status progression
- Ensure contrast for readability

## Language & Localization

### Supported Languages

RewardsPro supports multiple languages:
- English (default)
- French
- Spanish  
- German
