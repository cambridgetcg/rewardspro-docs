# Glossary

Key terms and definitions for RewardsPro.

## A

### Annual Evaluation
A tier calculation method where only spending from the past 12 months counts toward tier qualification.

### Audit Trail
A complete record of all changes to customer accounts, including who made changes and when.

## C

### Cashback
Store credit earned as a percentage of purchase amount, based on the customer's tier rate.

### Cashback Rate
The percentage of an order's eligible amount that's awarded as store credit.

## D

### Default Tier
The tier automatically assigned to new customers when they join the loyalty program.

### Double-Entry Ledger
An accounting system where every transaction has two entries (debit and credit) to maintain balance.

## E

### Eligible Amount
The portion of an order that qualifies for cashback calculation (typically subtotal minus exclusions).

### Evaluation Period
The timeframe used to calculate a customer's spending for tier qualification (annual or lifetime).

## L

### Lifetime Evaluation
A tier calculation method where all-time spending counts toward tier qualification.

### Loyalty Tier
A level in the rewards program that determines benefits like cashback rate.

## M

### Manual Credit
Store credit added by the merchant, not earned through purchases.

### Membership Product
See Tier Product.

## O

### Order Subtotal
The sum of line item prices before shipping, tax, and tips.

## P

### Progress
A customer's advancement toward the next tier, usually shown as a percentage.

## R

### Redemption
Using store credit to pay for a purchase.

### Redemption Rate
The percentage of issued store credit that has been used by customers.

## S

### Spending Threshold
The minimum amount a customer must spend to qualify for a tier.

### Store Credit
A balance customers can use toward future purchases, earned through cashback or added manually.

## T

### Tier
A level in the loyalty program with specific benefits (e.g., Bronze, Silver, Gold).

### Tier Product
A purchasable product that grants instant access to a specific tier for a set duration.

### Tier Upgrade
When a customer moves to a higher tier, either through spending or purchasing a tier product.

## W

### Webhook
An automated message sent from Shopify to RewardsPro when events occur (like order paid).

### Widget
The customer-facing interface that displays tier status, balance, and progress on the storefront.
