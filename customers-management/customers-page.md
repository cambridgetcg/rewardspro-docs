# Customers Page

<div data-with-frame="true"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Customers Page.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Customers currently registered in your shopify store can be synced and managed in the Customers Page of Rewards Pro, specifically regarding customer details, assigning tiers and editing store credits.
